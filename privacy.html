Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy describes how gdrive-backup ("the app") handles information. The app is a personal, single-user backup tool operated by the owner of the hughes5 GitHub account. It is not offered as a public service.

Information we collect

None. This website does not collect, store, or process personal data, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

Google user data

The app uses OAuth 2.0 to connect to the operator's own Google Drive account with the drive.file scope. This scope allows the app to create and manage only the files it creates; it does not grant access to other files in the account.

The app uploads restic backup repositories. Those repositories are encrypted on the backup server before upload, so the data stored in Google Drive is ciphertext. The app does not read, analyze, sell, or share Google user data, and does not use it for advertising.

Limited Use

gdrive-backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Backups remain in Google Drive until the operator deletes them or removes them through the app. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which invalidates the app's stored token. Encrypted backup data can be deleted from Google Drive by the operator.
